Applies to: `DPS310`, `HMC5883L-TR`, `ICM-45686`, `SAM-M10Q-00B`, `SX1280`.

The license shipped with these bundles (`SX1280/License.txt` in the source tree,
reproduced at [`LICENSES/snapeda.txt`](LICENSES/snapeda.txt)) allows using the models
to design boards, and allows conveying the resulting board designs under terms of
your choice, but forbids redistributing the models themselves, for example by
uploading them to another site or application. It also forbids removing the
copyright or proprietary notices inside the models.

This repository is a personal working copy that is published publicly, which is a
grey area at best under those terms. If you are not the owner, do not treat these
files as a redistributable library.
